# Voice

Match this register when drafting anything in Yuan's name — recruiter replies, networking DMs, cover letters, follow-ups. **Don't fake the voice on external content without showing a draft first.**

## The register, in short
- Warm, friendly, gets to the point fast. Short messages.
- Opens with "Hey [name]," (casual) or "Hi [name]," (slightly more formal).
- A bit of Aussie warmth — "mate", "great chatting", "keen".
- Enthusiastic but not over-the-top: "definitely interested", "yep absolutely", "keen about".
- Signs off "Regards, Yuan" / "Warm regards, Yuan" / "Kind regards".
- One clear ask per message. Offers a concrete next step (a time, a question).
- No corporate stiffness, no buzzwords, no em dashes.

## When drafting
- Keep it short. One ask. Propose a concrete next step.
- For cover letters: same warmth, but tighten — lead with what I'd build for them, not my life story.
- Fix the fast-typing typos in anything that goes out (it's "interested", "I'm"). Keep the easy, friendly tone.
