## Market Context

- ~20,000 students across ~400 Texas FFA chapters
- No standardized practice software exists — teachers historically use paper packets, Google Forms, or nothing
- FFA chapters decide independently; ag teacher/advisor is the buyer

---

## Known Competitors / Adjacent Tools

### Quizlet
- **What:** General flashcard/quiz platform
- **FFA use:** Some teachers create FFA-specific decks (e.g., Vet Science CDE Quizlet sets exist with 96K+ users)
- **Gaps vs Ag Coach Pro:** Not FFA-specific, no CDE contest simulation, no AI grading, no teacher dashboards, no oral reasons feedback
- **Relevant URLs found in resource audit:** https://quizlet.com/96211279/ffa-vet-science-cde-exam-questions-flash-cards/

### APlus Education (aplus.net)
- **What:** General education quiz and test prep platform
- **FFA use:** Sometimes referenced for CDE practice
- **Gaps vs Ag Coach Pro:** Not FFA/CDE specific; no AI features

### FlatHead / State FFA Association Tools
- **What:** Some state FFA associations (Texas, Iowa, Washington, Oklahoma) publish free PDF handbooks, past tests, and practice resources
- **Texas FFA CDE Hub:** https://www.texasffa.org/cde
- **Gaps vs Ag Coach Pro:** Static PDFs, no interactive practice, no AI feedback, no tracking

### Examzify
- **What:** Test prep platform with some FFA CDE content
- **Noted in resource audit:** https://ffa-vetscience-cdecomplete.examzify.com/
- **Gaps vs Ag Coach Pro:** Limited CDE coverage; not Texas-specific; no AI grading or teacher dashboards
