### 3. MEAT SCIENCE / MEATS EVALUATION & TECHNOLOGY CDE

- **National FFA Meats Evaluation and Technology CDE Handbook**

  - Available at: <https://www.ffa.org/resource_tag/cde-handbook-q-qnd-a-q-a-previous-tests-practicum-meats-evaluation-and-technology-cde-handbook/>
  - Covers rules, scoring, grading standards, past tests

- **Texas FFA Meats Evaluation Rules**

  - Location: Texas CDE Handbook 2022-2026

#### Event Format & Components

| Component                | Details                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Written Exam**         | Multiple-choice & matching on meat knowledge |
| **Quality Grading**      | Beef carcasses (marbling, maturity scores)   |
| **Yield Grading**        | Beef carcasses (fat thickness, ribeye area)  |
| **Cut Identification**   | Recognize meat cuts                          |
| **Processing Knowledge** | Labeling, safety, regulations                |

#### Grading Criteria

**USDA Quality Grades**:

- Prime, Choice, Select, Standard, Commercial

**Marbling Scores**:

- Abundant, moderately abundant, moderate, slight, traces, practically devoid

**Maturity Classes**:

- A, B, C, D, E (based on bone and muscle development)

**Yield Grades**:

- 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (based on cutability)

### 4. CREED SPEAKING (Leadership Development Event)

#### Official Rules & Handbooks

- **National FFA Creed Speaking Handbook**

  - Available at: <https://www.ksffa.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Creed-SpeakingHandbook.pdf>
  - Covers rules, format, judging criteria, FFA Creed text

- **Texas FFA Creed Speaking Rules**

  - Location: Texas FFA Handbook/LDE rules

#### Event Format

| Component            | Details                                            |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Creed Recitation** | 3-4 minutes (varies by state)                      |
| **Questions**        | 3 questions per round (5 min total)                |
| **Judging Criteria** | Delivery, content knowledge, poise, interpretation |
| **Format**           | Individual event                                   |
| **Dress Code**       | Official FFA dress                                 |

#### FFA Creed Text

- Must use current year's Official FFA Manual text
- Available in: <https://www.ffa.org/official-manual/>
- Memorization required

#### Practice Resources

✅ **Available**:

- Official FFA Creed text (annual)
- Judging rubrics from multiple states
- Sample questions from handbooks
- State association guidelines

⚠️ **Needed**:

- Video examples of excellent delivery
- Question bank (practice questions from past competitions)
- Delivery coaching tips (tone, pace, gesture)
- Interpretation guides
- Self-assessment rubrics
- Recording/playback tool for practice

#### Judging Criteria (Typical)

1. **Presentation** (40%)

   - Voice quality, pacing, enunciation
   - Eye contact, gesture, posture
   - Overall delivery

1. **Content Knowledge** (35%)

   - Accurate creed recitation
   - Understanding of FFA principles
   - Answer quality to questions

1. **Poise & Professionalism** (25%)

   - Composure under pressure
   - Response to difficult questions
   - Professional demeanor

#### Links to Source

- <https://nyageducation.squarespace.com/s/Senior-Creed-Speaking-Handbook-hk9n.pdf> (New York handbook)
- <https://neaged.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Creed-Speaking.pdf> (Nebraska rules)
