## Prompt Writing Rules (Bryan's Format)

For image/video prompts, use structured Gemini/Nano Banana Pro format:
1. **Subject** — who/what
2. **Action** — what they're doing
3. **Context** — where/when
4. **Composition** — framing, angle
5. **Lighting** — natural, golden hour, etc.
6. **Style** — photorealistic, cinematic, etc.
7. **Text constraints** — avoid text in image unless intentional
