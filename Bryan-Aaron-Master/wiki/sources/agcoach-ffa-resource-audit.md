# Ag Coach Pro — FFA Resource Audit (Gap Analysis)

**Date:** April 14, 2026. Comprehensive gap analysis for CDE/LDE content coverage across the 5 initial contest modules.

## Scope

Initial 5 CDEs/LDEs:
1. **Farm Business Management** — Content Pack created ✅
2. **Livestock Judging (Evaluation)** — Rules found, content needed ⚠️
3. **Veterinary Science** — Rules found, content needed ⚠️
4. **Meat Science (Meats Evaluation & Technology)** — Rules found, content needed ⚠️
5. **Creed Speaking** — Rules found, content needed ⚠️

## Official Sources

- **National FFA CDE Hub**: https://www.ffa.org/resource_tag/cde-handbooks/
- **Texas FFA CDE Hub**: https://www.texasffa.org/cde
- **Texas CDE Handbook 2022-2026** (CRITICAL): https://www.texasffa.org/docs/CDE%20Handbook%202022-2026%20-%2010.9.2025_61720.pdf
- **Texas A&M AgriLife Vet Science Test Bank (150 Qs)**: https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/asset-external/veterinary-science-cde-test-bank-2025/

## Contest-Specific Formats

**Livestock Judging:** Written test 25 MC (50 pts) + oral reasons. Team 3-4 members. Classes: beef cattle, sheep, swine, market goats.

**Veterinary Science:** Written test + clinical practicum + identification (diseases, anatomy, parasites, organs). Knowledge domains: large animal, small animal, clinical skills, disease/parasite ID.

**Meat Science:** Written exam + quality grading (beef carcasses) + yield grading + cut identification + processing knowledge. USDA grades: Prime/Choice/Select/Standard/Commercial. Marbling: Abundant→Practically Devoid. Maturity: A–E. Yield Grades: 1–5.

**Creed Speaking (LDE):** 3-4 min recitation + 3 questions (5 min total). Judging: Presentation 40%, Content Knowledge 35%, Poise & Professionalism 25%.

**Farm Business Management:** Content pack already exists. Needs: interactive calculator, case study scenarios, team practice templates.

## What Is Available vs. Needed

### Available ✅
- Texas CDE Handbook 2022-2026 (all events)

- Quizlet banks (Vet Science)

### High-Priority Gaps ⚠️
- Breed standards database (cattle, sheep, swine)
- USDA meat grading visual guides + carcass images
- Livestock anatomy diagrams (large + small animal)
- Parasite ID images + surgical instruments guide
- Creed Speaking question bank from past competitions
- Interactive scoring calculators (all events)
