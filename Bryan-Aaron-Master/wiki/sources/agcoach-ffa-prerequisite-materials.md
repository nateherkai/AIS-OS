# Ag Coach Pro — Prerequisite Materials Sourcing Guide

Sourcing instructions for key reference materials needed to build the Farm Business Management CDE module.

## Key Materials

### 1. Farm Management (9th Edition)
- **Authors**: Ronald D. Kay, William M. Edwards, Patricia A. Duffy
- **Publisher**: McGraw-Hill (2020), ISBN: 978-1-260-00219-5
- **Free access**: Internet Archive — https://archive.org/details/farmmanagement0000kayr (14-day borrow)
- **Sample chapters**: https://www.mheducation.com/unitas/highered/sample-chapters/9781260002195.pdf
- **Key chapters for FBM module**: 1 (Principles), 2 (Decision Making), 3 (Records), 4-8 (Financial Analysis), 9 (Risk Management)

### 2. Farm and Ranch Business Management (8th Edition)
- **Publisher**: John Deere Publishing (2017), ISBN: 0866914390
- **Free access**: FFA Box via https://www.ffa.org/resource_tag/cde-handbooks/ — official educational distribution
- **Backup**: John Deere Publishing direct contact for educator access
- **Key chapters**: 1-12 (business fundamentals)

### 3. Farm Business Management Content Pack
- Pre-extracted Markdown file with 12 sections, 50+ questions, financial ratios, risk management framework, 243 economics terms, case study templates
- Already formatted for Claude Code ingestion

## Ingestion Workflow (for Claude Code)

1. Borrow Kay/Edwards/Duffy from Internet Archive → extract chapters 1-9
2. Download John Deere 8e from FFA Box → extract chapters 1-12
3. Use all three materials + Content Pack as reference for Farm Business Management module

## Legal Status

All recommended sources are legal — Internet Archive lending, official FFA educational PDFs, publisher educational access. No piracy.
