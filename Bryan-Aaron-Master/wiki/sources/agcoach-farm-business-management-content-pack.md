# Ag Coach Pro — Farm Business Management CDE Content Pack

**Date:** April 14, 2026. Pre-extracted content pack for the FBM CDE module — 12 sections, 50+ questions, financial ratios, risk management framework, 243 economics terms, case study templates.

## Contest Format

- **Individual Activity:** 50-question MC test, 90 min, 300 pts (top 3 scores count for team)
- **Team Activity:** 25 open-ended problems, 75-90 min, 300 pts (100 teamwork + 200 answer sheet)
- **Total possible:** 1,200 pts
- 4-member team; no graphing calculators; no cell phones; concepts from National FFA Career Development Handbook only
- Tiebreaker: most correct on 10 pre-selected questions → highest problem-solving team score

## Core Concept Domains

### Economic Principles
- **Production functions:** diminishing returns, marginal revenue = marginal cost at profit max
- **Opportunity cost:** highest-valued alternative foregone
- **Cost types:** Fixed (interest, taxes, insurance, depreciation) vs Variable (feed, labor, seed, fuel). TC = VC + FC. AC = TC ÷ units. MC = ΔTC ÷ ΔQ
- **Enterprise selection:** Specialization (comparative advantage) vs Diversification (risk reduction)
- **Market types:** Perfect competition (price-taker, commodity ag), Monopolistic competition (differentiated, price-searcher), Oligopoly, Monopoly

### Supply, Demand & Equilibrium
- Law of Supply: direct price-quantity relationship (upward slope); Law of Demand: inverse (downward slope)
- Elasticity: elastic = quantity sensitive to price change; inelastic = quantity insensitive
- Equilibrium: Qs = Qd. Surplus when market price > equilibrium; Shortage when < equilibrium
- Substitutes vs Complements

### Financial Statements
- **Balance Sheet:** snapshot — Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity. Current assets (cash within 1 yr) vs Non-current (fixed)
- **Income Statement:** revenue − expenses = net income over a period
- **Cash Flow Statement:** tracks actual cash in/out
- Financial ratios: liquidity, solvency, profitability

### Risk Management
- Types: production, marketing, financial, legal/human, institutional
- Tools: crop insurance, diversification, forward contracts, futures hedging
- Decision matrices, expected value analysis
