# Ag Coach Pro — Meat Science (Meats Evaluation & Technology) CDE

## Contest Format

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Written Exam | MC + matching on meat knowledge |
| Quality Grading | Beef carcasses (marbling, maturity) |
| Yield Grading | Beef carcasses (fat thickness, ribeye area) |
| Cut Identification | Recognize meat cuts |
| Processing Knowledge | Labeling, safety, regulations |

## USDA Grading Standards

**Quality Grades**: Prime, Choice, Select, Standard, Commercial

**Marbling Scores**: Abundant, Moderately Abundant, Moderate, Slight, Traces, Practically Devoid

**Maturity Classes**: A, B, C, D, E (based on bone and muscle development)

**Yield Grades**: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (based on cutability/fat cover)
