# Ag Coach Pro — Creed Speaking LDE

## Contest Format

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Creed Recitation | 3-4 minutes |
| Questions | 3 questions per round (5 min total) |
| Format | Individual event |
| Dress Code | Official FFA dress |

## Judging Criteria

1. **Presentation (40%)** — Voice quality, pacing, enunciation, eye contact, gesture, posture, overall delivery
2. **Content Knowledge (35%)** — Accurate creed recitation, understanding of FFA principles, answer quality
3. **Poise & Professionalism (25%)** — Composure under pressure, response to difficult questions, professional demeanor

FFA Creed text must be from the current year's Official FFA Manual: https://www.ffa.org/official-manual/

## Official Resources

- Kansas handbook (excellent example): https://www.ksffa.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Creed-SpeakingHandbook.pdf
- Texas FFA LDE rules: https://www.texasffa.org/docs/Full%20LDE%20Approved%20Revisions%203Q22%20BOD_04.04.22_87687.pdf

## Content Gaps in Ag Coach Pro

- Video examples of excellent delivery
- Question bank from past competitions
- Delivery coaching tips (tone, pace, gesture)
- Interpretation guides
- Self-assessment rubrics
- Recording/playback tool for practice

Note: Spanish Creed Speaking module exists as a placeholder file — needs implementation following same rubrics.

## AI Scoring (Ag Skills / LDE Video Scorer)

Ag Coach Pro has a 1,000-point AI rubric for video speech submissions:
- Introduction: 100 pts
- Performance: 550 pts (content, accuracy, demonstration)
- Communication: 250 pts (delivery, pace, tone, body language)
- Summary: 100 pts

Target benchmarks: 400–550 (learning), 650–800 (competition-ready), 850+ (state finalist)

Platform limitation: Native video recording via `MediaRecorder` is unavailable on mobile — requires alternative approach.
