# Ag Coach Pro — Veterinary Science CDE

## Contest Format

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Written Test | Multiple-choice (core knowledge) |
| Clinical Practicum | Hands-on procedures/skills evaluation |
| Identification | Disease, anatomy, parasites, organs |
| Format | Individual and team components |

## Knowledge Domains

1. **Large Animal Medicine** — cattle, horses, swine: diseases, treatments, reproductive systems, nutrition
2. **Small Animal Medicine** — dogs, cats, small pets: anatomy/physiology, health conditions, care procedures
3. **Clinical Skills** — surgical instrument identification, cleaning/maintenance, basic procedures, safety protocols
4. **Disease & Parasite Identification** — internal parasites, external parasites, disease signs, organ identification

## Key Resources

- Official handbook: https://www.ffa.org/resource_tag/cde-handbook-q-qnd-a-q-a-previous-tests-practicum-veterinary-science-cde-handbook/
- Texas CDE Handbook 2022-2026 (Texas-specific rules)
- **Texas A&M AgriLife Vet Science Test Bank 2025 (150 questions)**: https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/asset-external/veterinary-science-cde-test-bank-2025/
- Quizlet banks: https://quizlet.com/96211279 (96 cards), https://quizlet.com/747429529 (747 cards)
