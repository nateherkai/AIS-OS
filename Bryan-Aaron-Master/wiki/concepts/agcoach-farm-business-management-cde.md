# Ag Coach Pro — Farm Business Management CDE

The FBM CDE teaches management skills, critical thinking, and economic analysis for agricultural businesses. It is the **only initial CDE with a pre-built content pack** (April 2026); the other 4 CDEs still need content compilation.

## Contest Structure

| Component | Format | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Individual MC test | 50 questions, 90 min | 300 pts (top 3 of 4 count = 900 team) |
| Team problem solving | 25 open-ended, 75-90 min | 100 teamwork + 200 answer sheet = 300 |
| **Total** | | **1,200 pts** |

4-member team. No graphing calculators. No cell phones. Official source: National FFA Career Development Handbook.

## Key Knowledge Areas

1. **Economic principles** — production functions, diminishing returns, opportunity cost, MC/MR analysis
2. **Enterprise selection** — specialization vs diversification tradeoffs
3. **Market types** — perfect competition (commodity ag), monopolistic, oligopoly, monopoly
4. **Financial statements** — balance sheet (Assets = Liabilities + Equity), income statement, cash flow
5. **Cost analysis** — Fixed vs Variable, TC/AC/MC formulas
6. **Supply & demand** — equilibrium, elasticity, substitutes/complements
7. **Risk management** — production/marketing/financial/legal risk; insurance, diversification, futures
