# Nate Herk

YouTube creator and AI educator. Runs the "Up-to-AI" channel focused on Claude Code, agentic workflows, and personal productivity systems. Built two Obsidian-based LLM Wiki vaults: one for his 36+ YouTube transcripts, one as a personal second brain for business/employees/Q2 initiatives.

## Notable Systems

- **YouTube Transcript Wiki:** 36 videos ingested in one batch (~14 min). Claude auto-extracted tools, techniques, concepts, people, comparisons — zero manual relationship-building. Used for querying his own content library.
- **Herc Brain (Personal Second Brain):** Business decisions, employee context, Q2 initiatives. Hot cache (`hot.md`) stores ~500 chars of most-recent context to reduce crawls. Connected to his executive assistant ("Herc 2") via `CLAUDE.md` wiki path pointer.
- **WAT Framework:** A named technique Herk references across multiple videos (crosslinks appear in his transcript graph).

## Approach to LLM Wikis

Herk advocates keeping two separate vaults for separate purposes (one per topic domain) rather than one megavault. He notes that the executive assistant token usage dropped after switching from context files to wiki-query pattern.
