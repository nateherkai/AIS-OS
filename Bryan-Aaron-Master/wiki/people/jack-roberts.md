# Jack Roberts

AI builder and YouTube educator. Former tech startup founder (built and sold a startup with "a gazillion customers"). Now builds AI businesses and documents what actually works on his YouTube channel. Self-described: "I just show you the stuff that actually works."

## What He Built

- **Claude Code OS Dashboard:** A visual HTML dashboard that unifies his entire AI stack — models, plans, memory, skills, knowledge, connections — into one interface. Includes a self-improving component via a Dreaming Engine.
- **Hermes Agent:** Previously built an agent that automatically detected repetitive tasks and converted them into skills. The Dreaming Engine in the CC-OS incorporates this idea.
- **Design Systems Resource:** Open repo codifying what good design looks like; the Dreaming Engine can surface this resource automatically if design work is detected.

## Teaching Style

Shows live demos with real data. Community-based course delivery (releases inside a paid community with a full Claude Code masterclass). Direct, builder-first communication style.
