# About the Business

**Ag Coach Pro** — AI-powered FFA CDE/LDE training platform at agcoachpro.com.

Sells annual site licenses to Texas FFA chapters. Primary buyer: ag teacher/advisor. Primary users: FFA students drilling for competitive events (CDEs). Secondary: parents tracking student progress.

**Tiers:**
- Greenhand — $495/yr (LDE team + individual quiz logins, 5M AI credits)
- Blue & Gold — $895/yr (+ 40 CDE logins, 15M credits)
- Lone Star Elite — $1,495/yr (unlimited seats, 40M credits)
- Feed Bags — $100/1M supplemental credits, rollover with active license

**Revenue lands in:** Stripe.

**Key features:** RAG-backed quiz generation from Texas FFA rulebooks, AI livestock ID drills (4-panel composite images), flashcards, Ag Coach Brain chatbot, teacher readiness dashboards, performance gap reports.

**Stack:** Expo Router / React Native / Supabase / Gemini AI. Runs web + mobile.

**Go-to-market:** In-person at Ag Teachers Conferences, FFA conventions, and events. Follow-up via email (support@agcoachpro.com / Gmail), MailerLite blasts, text, Facebook DMs.
