# About the Job Search (my "business")

This AIOS runs a job search, not a company. The "offer" is me as a candidate. The "customers" are companies hiring. The "revenue" is the opportunity pipeline — leads in, interviews through, offer out.

## The offer (me as a candidate)
Full-stack engineer with deep integrations + automation experience. Builds systems that connect tools and remove manual work (Intercom/Notion/Slack escalation flows, API-driven auto-listers, ETL + cost-optimisation pipelines). Comfortable across cloud (GCP, Azure, AWS), IaC, CI/CD, and data/BI. Pragmatic LLM user for prototyping and workflow automation. Pitch in one line: *the engineer who automates the boring parts so the team ships faster.*

## The ICP (who I'm selling to)
- **Roles:** Software Engineering or AI Engineering (esp. AI orchestration / workflow automation).
- **Company type:** startups preferred — more exciting, more ownership.
- **Location / mode:** fully remote preferred (open to international); hybrid OK if local to Melbourne.
- **Comp target:** $150K+ AUD plus super.

## The pipeline (where opportunities come from + how they're tracked)
- **Sources today:** Seek, LinkedIn. **Open to add:** Wellfound (AngelList), Y Combinator's Work at a Startup, Hacker News "Who's Hiring", Indeed.
- **Inbound:** recruiters via LinkedIn (already a steady channel — see voice samples).
- **Tracking:** markdown in this repo at `projects/job-search/applications.md`. No external PM tool.

## What "winning" looks like this quarter
A robust, mostly-automated search that surfaces 10+ quality roles a week, tailors each application, and converts a shortlist of 20 target startups into warm intros and interviews. See `context/priorities.md`.
